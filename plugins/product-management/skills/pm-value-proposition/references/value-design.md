# Value design and messaging

Source: Paweł Huryn and Aatir Abdul Rauf, *How to Design a Value Proposition Customers Can't Resist?*, April 14, 2024. Source originals are not included; use the bundled guidance and Markdown worksheets.

## Develop the reasoning
Use one segment and context, identify the job, describe the existing situation, connect the enabling capability to a desired outcome, and compare alternatives. The internal proposition supports team alignment; reusable customer-facing statements are a separate output.

When the job is broad, break it into relevant steps and desired improvements. Trace important capabilities to the steps and outcomes they support. Prioritize using customer evidence and consequences; do not imply all pains and benefits matter equally or invent priority ratings. Use the [working sheet](../templates/value-proposition.md): draft one row together, explain its links, then expand only when helpful.

## Develop messaging when requested
Draft a few candidate statements for the chosen audience and touchpoint. Each should identify the useful change and enough of how it works to make the promise concrete. A vague benefit lacks a mechanism; a feature list lacks customer consequences. Record the benefit, enabling capability, claim/evidence status, alternative and intended use. Recommend one with rationale while keeping unproven benefits labelled as hypotheses.

Use pm-discovery to check comprehension, relevance and credibility with intended users. Separately test whether customers choose, adopt and obtain the promised outcome. Message preference or clicks alone do not establish product value. Adapt wording across onboarding, landing pages and other touchpoints while keeping the underlying proposition consistent. Iterate when evidence changes.

## Source boundaries
The authors' criticism of Strategyzer is a viewpoint, not proof that its canvas cannot support a single product or prioritization. Preserve an existing representation when it is useful. Canva and Close examples are historical illustrations, not current competitive research or verified outcomes. Neither persuasive copy nor the article title guarantees adoption. Evidence labels and explicit experiment criteria are additions made by this suite.
