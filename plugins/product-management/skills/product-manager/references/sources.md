# Public sources and adaptation notes

This suite is adapted and maintained by Stylus Nexus. ProductCompass guidance by Paweł Huryn and collaborators informs the workflows; source names identify attribution, not plugin authorship. The suite adds evidence labels, uncertainty, proportionate coaching, project isolation, authorization boundaries and explicit verification limits. Original articles, slide decks, PDFs and private review records are not included. Use bundled Markdown worksheets instead.

## Additional attributed guidance

Individual reference files retain author, title and date for ProductCompass material on discovery sources, Kano, discovery streams, product risks, PRDs, user stories, OKRs, competency coaching, interviews, journey mapping, A/B testing, North Star metrics, metrics definitions, leadership, automation and outcome roadmaps. These are operational adaptations, not reproductions of the source articles or claims to have read every linked work. Books such as TRANSFORMED and The Right It are not bundled or reproduced.

The framework references are not guaranteed current market facts. Published company strategy examples are historical teaching illustrations, not authenticated internal strategies or current competitive intelligence. Verify time-sensitive claims against current primary evidence. Do not adopt fixed metric quotas, invented scores, unsupported causal claims or synthetic research as customer validation.

## Framework differences

Huryn's canvas combines vision and winning aspiration and questions separate mission statements. Treat that as a framework choice, not a universal definition. Support useful mission/vision distinctions without requiring redundant documents. The Blue Ocean strategy canvas is a value curve; it is distinct from the broader Product Strategy Canvas. Capability guidance is a practical synthesis, not enterprise-architecture certification.
