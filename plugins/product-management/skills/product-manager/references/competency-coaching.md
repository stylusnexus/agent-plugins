# Optional competency-informed coaching

Informed by Paweł Huryn, *Product Manager Competencies Map + Skills Assessment*, September 16, 2023. Its linked assessment and interactive XMind map were not accessed, and no score/certification is claimed.

Use eight topic areas as a menu for supporting the current work:

| Area | Suite route | Useful practice artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Business and strategy | pm-vision, pm-strategy, pm-canvas | Explain a strategic choice and sacrifice |
| Execution | pm-objectives, pm-roadmap | Link one initiative to an outcome |
| People | pm-teams and chief coaching | Clarify ownership or work through a disagreement |
| Data analytics | pm-objectives, pm-growth; available analytics skills | Define and interpret a metric with limitations |
| Discovery | pm-discovery | Trace evidence to a problem and alternatives |
| Experimentation | pm-discovery | Design a decision-changing test |
| Product marketing | pm-value-proposition, pm-growth | Explain value to a specific audience |
| Growth | pm-growth | Diagnose a constraint and propose a bounded test |

Default to helping with the user's current project, not testing them. Notice a concept that would help, explain it at that moment, draft together and reduce scaffolding with familiarity. A non-PM need not master all eight areas to make progress.

When the user asks for a learning plan or self-assessment, ask which real decisions they want to improve. Use their chosen work samples and self-reflection to identify one or two useful practice areas. Mark unfamiliar, practiced with help, or demonstrated independently only with evidence and user context; do not infer competence from vocabulary or fabricate a numeric rating. Offer a small exercise, feedback and a revisit point. Formal quizzes or tutor mode are opt-in.

The article's role-level expectations are the author's context-dependent judgment. Do not treat them as hiring standards, certification, a universal PM career ladder or proof of future salary/market-value gains. Respect specialists: route deep analysis, UX or engineering work when available while keeping teaching and synthesis with the chief. Do not force the user to behave as an entire staffed product organization.
