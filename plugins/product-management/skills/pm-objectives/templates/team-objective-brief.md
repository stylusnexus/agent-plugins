# Team objective — [name]

- **Strategic context:** mission/aspiration, target segment/job, where-to-play/how-to-win choice.
- **Problem and why now:** evidence, consequence, and why this matters more than alternatives.
- **Objective:** meaningful improvement to achieve.
- **Success measures:** key results with definition, source, baseline/date, target/rationale, timeframe; label unknowns.
- **Guardrails:** what must not deteriorate while pursuing the outcome.
- **Boundaries:** strategic trade-offs, non-goals, constraints, accepted risks.
- **Autonomy:** choices the team owns; unresolved strategic choices retained by the owner.
- **Dependencies:** other teams/capabilities/data and coordination needed.
- **Discovery:** critical assumptions, experiments, and open solution options.
- **Accountability:** owner, review cadence, learning record, continue/change/stop triggers.

Keep the objective separate from candidate initiatives and delivery milestones. Proposed metrics and targets are not accepted commitments until agreed.
