# Product model first principles: practical application

Source orientation: Paweł Huryn / The Product Compass, [Product Model First Principles and Cagan Q&A](https://www.productcompass.pm/p/product-model-first-principles-transformed-cagan). This is an operational adaptation, not a full-book extraction of TRANSFORMED. Original graphics are not reproduced.

| Area | Principle from the source framework | Apply it proportionately |
|---|---|---|
| Team | Empowered with Problems to Solve | Give a consequential customer or company problem, strategic context, and authority to explore solutions. |
| Team | Outcomes over Output | Evaluate beneficial change; track shipped work separately. |
| Team | Sense of Ownership | Keep responsibility for learning, delivery, operation, and impact visible. |
| Team | Collaboration | Combine relevant perspectives and evidence; resolve disagreement without requiring consensus. |
| Strategy | Focus | Select important problems and decline attractive distractions. |
| Strategy | Powered by Insights | Ground choices in customer, product, technical, and market evidence. |
| Strategy | Transparency | Explain choices, evidence, trade-offs, and decision ownership. |
| Strategy | Placing Bets | Treat commitments as uncertain; define what learning changes the decision. |
| Discovery | Minimize Waste | Test important uncertainties before costly investment; don't add ceremony to verified simple work. |
| Discovery | Assess Product Risks | Inspect value, usability, viability, and feasibility assumptions. |
| Discovery | Embrace Rapid Experimentation | Choose small informative tests and clear decision criteria. |
| Discovery | Test Ideas Responsibly | Protect people and trust; honor consent, scope, and authorization. |
| Delivery | Small, Frequent, Uncoupled Releases | Prefer bounded changes where feasible; inspect coupling and rollback needs. |
| Delivery | Instrumentation | Define how outcomes and guardrails will be observed. |
| Delivery | Monitoring | Detect production problems and assign response ownership. |
| Delivery | Deployment Infrastructure | Identify the release capability needed for reliable delivery; route engineering work to the existing workflow. |
| Culture | Principles over Process | Adapt methods to the problem; don't confuse following a template with success. |
| Culture | Trust over Control | Provide context and decision rights without abandoning accountability or necessary controls. |
| Culture | Innovation over Predictability | Acknowledge uncertain discovery outcomes; keep explicit constraints and responsible delivery commitments. |
| Culture | Learning over Failure | Examine evidence from unsuccessful bets and change behavior without blame or hiding harm. |

For an assessment, record observation, relevant principle, impact, proposed change, owner, and evidence to review. Avoid a spurious maturity score or claiming the source proves every suggested practice.
