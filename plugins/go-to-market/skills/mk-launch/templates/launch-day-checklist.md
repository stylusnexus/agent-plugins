# Launch-day checklist

Use alongside [launch-plan.md](launch-plan.md) — this is the day-of execution list, not the planning document.

## Before the post goes up

- [ ] Touchpoint audit rows all marked ready, or each remaining gap has an owner and a known-gap note (see [touchpoint audit](../references/touchpoint-audit.md))
- [ ] Platform rules re-fetched today, not reused from a prior launch (see [platform rules](../references/platform-rules.md))
- [ ] Post copy pre-publish-checked (route through `mk-copy`)
- [ ] No upvote/comment asks anywhere in the post or planned comments — this is against the rules on Show HN and Product Hunt alike
- [ ] Whoever is answering comments today is actually available, not double-booked

## Platform-specific, at post time

- [ ] **Show HN:** the thing being shown is usable by a stranger right now
- [ ] **Product Hunt:** maker's first comment drafted and ready to post immediately after launch, introducing the product and asking for feedback, not upvotes
- [ ] **Reddit:** this specific subreddit's current sidebar/wiki rules checked today; posting to one community at a time, not the same link across several at once

## During launch

- [ ] Comments/replies monitored on a fixed cadence — write the cadence here: [every __ minutes for the first __ hours]
- [ ] Technical questions answered with specifics, not a defended pitch
- [ ] Anything broken gets triaged live: [who, and where the incident gets tracked]

## End of day

- [ ] Numbers captured for the post-launch readout (see [post-launch-readout.md](post-launch-readout.md)) — capture them today; a channel's numbers get noisier to reconstruct the longer you wait
- [ ] Known gaps from the readiness gate revisited: still open, or closed?
