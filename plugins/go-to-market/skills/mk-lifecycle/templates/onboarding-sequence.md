## For each email, before it ships

- **What real sentence of the product's own copy was this drafted from?** [README line, pricing copy, founder phrasing — if none exists yet, say so and default to the plainest version rather than inventing a voice]
- **Any claim that reads as a promise ("saves you hours," "the best way to")?** [needs a fact behind it, or it comes out]
- **Route through `mk-copy`'s pre-publish check before sending.**

## What happens if the trigger never fires

For each email keyed to "did X," name what happens to a contact who never does X — a specific fallback message, or an explicit decision to leave them where they are. Don't leave this unanswered; an untriggered sequence step that nobody re-checks is exactly the "stage nobody can observe" problem from the lifecycle map.

## Reading the results

One send is one data point — don't rewrite the sequence off a single email's numbers. Read clicks, conversions, replies, and unsubscribes together; treat open rate as context only (see [brand voice and the email loop](../references/brand-voice-email-loop.md) on why). Wait for a pattern across two or three sends before concluding the voice — not just a subject line — needs to change.
