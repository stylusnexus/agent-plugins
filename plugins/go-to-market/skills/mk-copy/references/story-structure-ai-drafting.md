This file is a pointer, not a duplicate; don't let the two drift.

## How stories fit into copy specifically
Use a story to earn the claim behind a headline or landing-page section, on landing pages and README openers as much as social posts. Fact-check every detail against real product behavior or a real conversation before it goes into published copy; delete anything invented — draft it, check every beat against what actually happened, then run it through the applicable voice check before it ships. Route the finished copy through the same byline rule as the full guide: a personal story runs only under the byline of the person it happened to, with their approval; company pieces use the organization's own voice — never name, quote, or describe a private individual on a public surface without their explicit approval for that surface.
