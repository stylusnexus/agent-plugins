# Monthly readout

Route the write-up through [the question router](../references/question-router.md): answer descriptive first, diagnostic only where the descriptive number moved enough to ask, and treat predictive or prescriptive claims as needing more support than a single month's data gives.

## Descriptive — what happened

Pull straight from [the scorecard](scorecard.md).

| Stage | Metric | This month | Last month | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

## Diagnostic — why, where a number actually moved

For each metric that moved enough to ask about: segment by channel, day, or cohort until the movement traces to something specific. Don't skip straight to a prescriptive answer from a descriptive number alone.

| Metric that moved | Segmented by | What the movement traces to |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

## Predictive — only where there's a real trend to read

A two- or three-week trend from a small or pre-launch product is not a forecast. Say so if that's what this is.

- **Trend worth naming:** [or: not enough data yet]

## Prescriptive — what to do, and why it's earned

Only a diagnostic answer should lead to a recommendation here.

- **Recommendation:** [tied to the diagnostic row it comes from]
- **What we're explicitly not changing this month, and why:** [worth recording — it's as much a decision as a change]

## Charting this readout

Match the chart to the message, not the other way round — comparison → bar chart (zero-based axis, not a pie past five categories); change over time → line chart, labelled directly rather than with a legend where possible; avoid merging two metrics onto one chart with two different y-axes. See [the question router](../references/question-router.md) for the sourced reasoning.

## Example (fictional)

| Stage | Metric | This month | Last month | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | signups | 340 | 212 | +60% |

Diagnostic: segmented by channel — the increase traces almost entirely to one referral source, not a broad lift. Prescriptive: double down on that one source before assuming the overall funnel improved.
