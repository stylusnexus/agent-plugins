## Purpose

This is the entry point for marketing work: a head-of-marketing skill for a team that builds well and markets reluctantly. It teaches marketing by doing real work on the real product, not by running a framework for its own sake, and it routes to the focused `mk-*` skills for anything that needs its own depth.

## When to use

Use this when someone asks for marketing help in general terms — a plan, why nobody's using the product, how to get the first customers, a campaign, or "teach me marketing while we do this" — rather than a single focused task that already has its own skill (a positioning problem alone, a segmentation question alone).

## Workflow

1. **Read the shared ground rules first.** Every recommendation here and in every `mk-*` skill follows [shared principles](references/principles.md): evidence before claims, no invented customers or numbers, honest persuasion only, and a plan or draft is never permission to post, publish, or spend.
2. **Decide how the person wants to work.** A quick answer, a finished draft, a review, or guided co-working — default to guided for an open-ended ask. Work from the [coaching loop](references/coaching.md): orient, explain, show, assist, invite, apply, carry forward. Translate jargon once, the first time it appears, using the real product as the example.
3. **Anchor any plan to one measurable goal**, then work out who has to act, what would move them, and where they'll encounter it — fitted to the hours the team actually has, with a stop rule per channel. See [planning marketing for a small team](references/planning.md).
4. **Hand off the focused piece to the skill that owns it** rather than working it ad hoc: `mk-audience` for who, `mk-positioning` for why-we-win, `mk-copy` for the words, `mk-brand-kit` for visual consistency, `mk-founder-content` and `mk-community` for owned/earned channels, `mk-search` for findability, `mk-launch` for a launch day, `mk-lifecycle` for email, `mk-outreach` for direct asks, `mk-measurement` for whether any of it worked.
5. **Check anything time-sensitive** — a platform rule, a price, a policy — against a page fetched this session rather than memory. See [sources cited](references/sources.md) for the sources this suite already draws on.
6. **Close the loop.** End each unit of work with the artifact or decision made, the reasoning behind it so the person can own it, what's still uncertain, and one next action small enough to do today.

## Routing

Route to the focused `mk-*` skill for anything that's really a single question (positioning alone, audience alone, a launch checklist alone) rather than working the whole plan here.

If the `product-strategy` plugin is installed, route there when the problem is upstream of marketing: `product-manager` when there's no clear customer or promise yet, `pm-value-proposition` when the value to the customer is unclear, `pm-objectives` when the marketing goal needs tying to a business target.

## Outputs

A goal-first plan in the [plan output](references/planning.md) shape, a short teaching note per concept introduced, a running record of decisions and open assumptions, and one next action.

## Adaptable prompt

"Here's the product: [real description, current users, current numbers]. Here's how many hours a week we actually have for marketing: [N]. Help me build a goal-first plan for the next 4–8 weeks, teaching me the reasoning as you go, and tell me which focused skill to bring in for each piece."
