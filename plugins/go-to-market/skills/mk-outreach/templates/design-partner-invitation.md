## Example (fictional)

*Illustration only — names, company, and details below are invented, not a real contact.*

- **Recipient:** Dana Ruiz, Head of Ops, Fictional Freight Co.
- **Country:** United States
- **Path:** cold (email)
- **One verified fact:** Dana posted on LinkedIn last month about manually reconciling delivery exceptions in a spreadsheet every Friday.
- **Offer:** a free, hands-on setup of our exception-tracking tool for her team, done by us, this week — not a demo.
- **Legal-floor checklist:** all checked — sender identity accurate, US recipient (CAN-SPAM), opt-out honored, domain authenticated.
- **Message:** "Hi Dana — I'm [name] at [company]; we build exception-tracking tools for freight ops teams, and I'm reaching out to a small number of ops leads directly rather than running an ad. Your Friday-reconciliation post stuck with me because it's the exact failure mode we designed around. Rather than ask for a demo call, I'd rather just set the tool up for your team myself this week, free, and let the work speak. Open to it, or is this the wrong moment? Either answer is fine — just let me know."
