1 · Product Strategy Canvas

S1 · Winning Aspiration

Scenario choice: help volunteer hosts run welcoming workshops without burning out.

S2 · Where to Play

Scenario choice: recurring local workshops with volunteer organizers, not municipal or corporate event planning.

S3 · How to Win

Hypothesis: reusable curated templates cut setup and support effort more than a generic document tool.

S4 · Capabilities

Required: plan curation, host onboarding, and participant communication.

S5 · Management Systems

Proposed: a recurring template-review cadence and a host feedback loop.

S6 · Key Measures

Proposed: measure completed usable plans; baseline unknown.

S7 · Open Assumptions

Hypothesis: host-to-host sharing helps relevant groups discover the tool; untested. Unknown: no established imitation barrier or incentive conflict against a copycat.

Section structure: Roger Martin and A.G. Lafley's Playing to Win choice cascade (winning aspiration, where to play, how to win, capabilities, management systems) plus this plugin's own key-measures/open-assumptions section — see the canvas template and its cited public sources. Example content and layout added for teaching.

Analysis: S2 narrows variation and may support S3, but that reinforcement is a hypothesis. S5's review cadence exists to keep S3 true over time; S7 remains unknown. Test the promised time saving before treating the canvas as validated.
