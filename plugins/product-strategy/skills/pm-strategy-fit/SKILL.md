# Product Strategy Fit

Read [shared principles](../product-manager/references/principles.md) before applying this workflow.

## Purpose
Evaluate whether strategic choices work together and can plausibly succeed. Distinguish **strategy fit** (coherence and reinforcement among choices and activities) from **product-market fit** (evidence of market demand and sustained value). Neither proves the other.

## Three required checks
1. **Can competitors copy it, and would they want to?** Identify the relevant rivals and substitutes. Separate a resource/capability barrier from an incentive or business-model conflict. State evidence, time horizon, cost to imitate, and counterarguments. Distinctive wording, generic AI, and being first do not establish defensibility. If the advantage is readily copied, say so without automatically rejecting a useful product.
2. **Do the choices reinforce each other?** Trace segment → value proposition → cost/price choices → channels/growth → capabilities → trade-offs → metrics. For each important link explain the mechanism, evidence, and tension. Distinguish simple consistency from active reinforcement. Identify competing incentives, unsupported promises, and capacity/economic contradictions.
3. **What must be true, and how will we test it?** Identify customer behavior, market/channel, economic, capability, usability, and feasibility assumptions. Prioritize consequences of being wrong and uncertainty. Define smallest useful tests and pass/fail/inconclusive decisions through `pm-discovery`.

## Workflow
- Read the current strategy/canvas and accepted decisions. Review the evidence independently; do not infer fit from every box being filled.
- Build a compact relationship table: choice A, choice B, reinforcing/conflicting/unknown, mechanism, evidence, consequence, suggested change.
- Use an activity-system diagram when it clarifies the mechanisms. Keep claimed and validated links visibly distinct.
- Stress-test a plausible rival response and a resource constraint. Check whether trade-offs remain credible when pressure rises.
- Report findings in order of consequence. Distinguish a contradiction, a missing capability, and an untested assumption; they require different responses.
- Recommend proceed with stated risks, revise a choice, or test before commitment. Do not invent a universal strategy-fit score or imply a clean review proves future success.

## Output
- Verdict and strongest evidence, with limits.
- Reinforcing relationships and contradictions.
- Can't-copy versus won't-copy analysis and horizon.
- Assumptions, tests, and changes needed.
- One short explanation of the most consequential relationship for the user.

## Adaptable prompt
“Review this strategy for internal fit, defensibility, and testable assumptions. Show how choices reinforce or contradict one another, distinguish competitors' inability from unwillingness to copy, and identify what must be true. Recommend specific changes or tests without claiming that coherence proves demand.”
